## 6. How we think about this

- Head: 030 — THE STATEMENT · HOW WE THINK ABOUT THIS
- Subhead: Short, honest, and up front: harm reduction, sources cited, not
  medical advice.
- HARM REDUCTION — We meet people where they are, assume some will proceed
  regardless, and help them do it more safely. Accurate dosing math is the
  central harm-reduction feature — dosing errors are the single biggest risk
  in self-administration.
- SOURCES CITED — Every per-peptide guide cites its sources. Sourced,
  transparent guidance is what separates this from anonymous forum threads.
- NOT MEDICAL ADVICE — PepTiter is educational only. It is explicitly not
  medical advice — stated up front and visibly, never buried as fine print.
- REFERENCE, NOT PRESCRIBER — The site shows common protocols and calculates
  yours. It never recommends or prescribes a dose.
- 031 TWO CATEGORIES, NEVER BLURRED — GLP-1s like semaglutide and tirzepatide
  are prescription medications. Many other peptides are sold as research
  chemicals not approved for human consumption — they aren't the same thing,
  and nothing on this site implies they are. · Tags: `[RX MEDICATION]`
  `[RESEARCH CHEMICAL — NOT FOR HUMAN USE]`
- 032 BEYOND THE MATH · SOURCING & PURITY — Fake and contaminated product is
  one of the biggest real risks in this space. Guidance on third-party lab
  testing and safe sourcing is a core trust and safety feature, not an add-on.
  · Rows: STERILE TECHNIQUE — A harm-reduction concern beyond dosing math. ·
  REFRIGERATED AFTER RECONSTITUTION — Storage matters once a vial is
  reconstituted. · THIRD-PARTY PURITY TESTING — Verify what's in the vial
  before it goes anywhere near a syringe.
