025 — Reconstitution

Reconstitution reference

Peptides come as lyophilized (freeze-dried) powder and must be reconstituted with bacteriostatic water before use. The ratios below are owner-supplied; the calculator does the exact math on your own numbers.

Owner-supplied Updated 2026-07

001 — Standard ratio

Standard ratio

2 mg vialAdd 1 mL bacteriostatic water.
5 mg vialAdd 2 mL — 100 units on a U-100 syringe then equals 5 mg (20 units = 1 mg).
10 mg vialAdd 2 mL — 100 units equals 10 mg (10 units = 1 mg).
15–120 mg vialAdd 2 mL to all — 100 units equals the vial's total mg.
International units (HGH, HCG)Add 1 mL — 100 units equals the vial's total IU.
Pre-mixed liquid blendsDo not reconstitute — draw directly (0.5 mL = 50 units).

002 — Conversion chart

Dosage conversion

Example: 1 mL water into a 5 mg vial (U-100 syringe).

10 units0.5 mg
20 units1.0 mg
50 units2.5 mg

003 — Exceptions

Exceptions

NAD+ (NJ1000)1000 mg vial — add 4 mL so the solution isn't too thick for a standard needle.
CagriSema (CS10)5 mg + 5 mg — treat as a 10 mg vial; add 2 mL.
BPC-157 + TB-500 (BB10/BB20)5 mg + 5 mg — treat as a 10 mg vial; add 2 mL.
GHK-Cu (CU100)100 mg vial — add 2 mL; 10 units = 10 mg.
SNAP-8 (NP810)10 mg vial — add 2 mL.

004 — Handling

Handling

Inject against the glassAim the water at the inside wall of the vial, not directly at the powder.
Let it dissolveSit 5–10 minutes at room temperature; swirl gently, never shake — shaking can damage the peptide bonds.
Store coldRefrigerate at 2–8 °C and use within 7–14 days.
Bacteriostatic, not sterileUse bacteriostatic water (0.9% benzyl alcohol) for multi-use vials; sterile water is only for immediate single use.

005 — Supplies

Supplies

Bacteriostatic Water 0.9% benzyl alcohol preservative — holds a reconstituted vial in the fridge for up to 14 days. BA3 · BA10
Acetic Acid 0.6% A solvent used for peptides that need it. AA3 · AA10
