001 — What it is

What it is

Semaglutide is a GLP-1 receptor agonist. GLP-1s like semaglutide and tirzepatide are prescription medications — a different category from peptides sold as research chemicals, and this guide never blurs the two.

Reconstitution

Lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides are reconstituted with bacteriostatic water and typically dosed via an insulin syringe measured in units. The calculator turns your vial's concentration and target dose into syringe units — no signup needed.
