What this does

The calculator does unit math on the values you enter — it converts concentration and target dose into syringe units. It does not originate protocol recommendations. Lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides are reconstituted with bacteriostatic water and typically dosed via an insulin syringe measured in units.
