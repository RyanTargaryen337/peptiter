030 — The statement

How we think about this

Short, honest, and up front: harm reduction, sources cited, not medical advice.

Harm reduction We meet people where they are, assume some will proceed regardless, and help them do it more safely. Accurate dosing math is the central harm-reduction feature — dosing errors are the single biggest risk in self-administration.
Sources cited Every per-peptide guide cites its sources. Sourced, transparent guidance is what separates this from anonymous forum threads.
Not medical advice PepTiter is educational only. It is explicitly not medical advice — stated up front and visibly, never buried as fine print.
Reference, not prescriber The site shows common protocols and calculates yours. It never recommends or prescribes a dose.

031 — The category line

Two categories, never blurred

GLP-1s like semaglutide and tirzepatide are prescription medications. Many other peptides are sold as research chemicals not approved for human consumption — they aren't the same thing, and nothing on this site implies they are.
